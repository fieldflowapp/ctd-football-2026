2. Competition Format

In both Intermediate and Senior, one group of four teams will play a round-robin format. The top two teams will play a final to determine first place.

Points System

ResultPoints
Win3 points
Draw1 point
Loss0 points

Tie-breakers - Two Teams

  1. Head-to-head result
  2. Better goal difference
  3. More goals scored
  4. Fair Play - total number of red and yellow cards
  5. Draw

Tie-breakers - Three or More Teams

  1. Better goal difference
  2. More goals scored
  3. Draw

3. Laws of the Game

FIFA Laws of the Game will apply, together with the tournament-specific regulations listed below.

  1. Matches will last 30 minutes, played as one continuous period with no change of ends.
  2. The referee will be responsible for starting and ending each match.
  3. Each team may include a maximum of 20 players.
  4. A team may start a match with a minimum of 7 players. If fewer than 7 players are present, the team will lose by walkover and the opponent will be awarded a 3-0 win.
  5. Substitutions must be authorised by the referee and will operate as rolling substitutions. The substitute may enter only after the replaced player has left the field, at the halfway line.
  6. Each student may take part in only one category. No changes to a team list will be accepted once that team has played its first match.
  7. A player who is sent off may not take any further part in the tournament.
  8. Any team that fails to appear at the scheduled time will lose the match. If neither team appears, both teams will lose the match and may be sanctioned with loss of points or qualification, with an adverse score of 0-2 recorded.
  9. If play is suspended temporarily to attend to an injured player or because the ball is lost, the referee must stop the clock. The referee is solely responsible for timekeeping.
  10. Each school is advised to bring at least two sets of shirts. If colours are similar, the change will be decided by draw. If the similarity remains, the organisers will provide bibs at the Central Table.
  11. Shin guards are compulsory in all categories and this rule will be strictly enforced by referees.
  12. Player eligibility: Intermediate - students in 1º and 2º Medio, born in 2010 or 2011. Senior - Open category. Over-age students must play in the next category above.
  13. The tournament will be played with a size 5 ball.

4. Referees

A team of referees has been contracted to officiate all matches in both categories.

  1. Referees must arrive at 08:15.
  2. Each referee will know in advance which field they will officiate on throughout all phases of the tournament.
  3. Each referee must report the result of every match to the organisers immediately after it ends.
  4. The tournament committee has organised a pre-tournament meeting with the referees to ensure full understanding of the Laws of the Game and tournament rules, particularly those related to Fair Play and student development.
  5. Referees must bring a whistle and cards and officiate in appropriate clothing: shorts and two shirts in case of colour similarity with the teams.

5. Additional Important Information

  1. Changing rooms will be available to participating schools for transit use only. Clothing and bags must not be left in these areas. Personal belongings should remain close to each school's chosen base area. The organisers accept no responsibility for lost items.
  2. Participating schools are asked to help keep the sports fields clean.
  3. A first-aid room and a nurse for pitch-side care will be available. An ambulance from Clínica Alemana will also be present throughout the day.
  4. Each school is responsible for providing lunch or snacks for its students.
  5. Schools are advised to bring their own water containers for student hydration.
  6. Bus parking will be available inside the school grounds.
  7. Schools may set up a tent on the sports fields if they wish. It may not display advertising or publicity unrelated to the school.
  8. If a Pre-Emergency environmental alert is declared, the tournament will be cancelled.

IMPORTANT: Animals are not allowed in the sports field area. Smoking is prohibited on school grounds.
