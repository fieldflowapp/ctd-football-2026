Official Rules

Date: Saturday, 26 September 2026.

The Charles T. Darling Girls’ Volleyball Tournament 2026 brings together Craighouse, Santiago College, The Grange and Dunalastair Valle Norte at the Craighouse gym, using 3 courts simultaneously.

Format

  • Round-robin format: 3 matches per team in each category.
  • Win: 3 points.
  • Loss: 1 point.
  • Walkover: 0 points.
  • 1st and 2nd place advance to the Grand Final.

Tie-break criteria

  1. Highest number of standings points.
  2. Best set difference.
  3. Best point difference.
  4. Head-to-head result between the tied teams.

Match rules

  • Best of 3 sets.
  • Set 1 and Set 2 are played to 21 points.
  • The tie-break set is played to 15 points.
  • Every set must be won by a 2-point margin.
  • 6 starting players + a maximum of 6 substitutes.
  • Separate rosters for Intermediate and Senior.
  • Official numbered uniform required.
  • If the ball touches the ceiling on the first or second contact and remains playable on the same side, play continues.
  • Fair Play is mandatory.

Awards

  • 1st place: Cup and gold medals.
  • 2nd place: Silver medals.
